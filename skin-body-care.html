Hair Removal

Threading

100% natural hair removal using cotton thread. Delivers sharp, defined brows and smooth skin without chemicals or heat. Ideal for sensitive skin types.

Liposoluble Wax

Premium waxing treatment enriched with natural resins and essential oils. Removes even short, stubborn hair with less discomfort, delays regrowth, and leaves skin soft, nourished, and smooth.

Hard Wax

A gentle, strip-free waxing method that hardens on the skin and is removed without cloth strips. Ideal for sensitive areas like face, underarms, and bikini, offering precise hair removal with reduced discomfort.

Sugar Wax

A chemical-free alternative to traditional waxing. This 100% natural sugar paste adheres only to the hair, not the skin, making it less painful, eco-friendly, and suitable for sensitive skin.
