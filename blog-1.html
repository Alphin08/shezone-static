HAIRCARE TREATMENT

Get Smooth, Frizz-Free Hair

Heat and daily styling leave your hair dry and frizzy. A keratin treatment refills lost protein and seals every strand for soft, effortless shine.

Published August 18, 2026
Reading time 3 min read

INTRODUCTION THE PROCEDURE AFTERCARE IS IMPORTANT A SMOOTH FINISH THAT LASTS

More than 90% of your hair is made up of a protein called keratin. The remaining part consists mainly of moisture and minerals. Keratin acts as a structural protein at the core of the hair, helping to keep it strong, smooth and healthy.

Over time, hair can lose keratin due to environmental factors such as extreme weather, heat and exposure to chemicals. As keratin is lost, the hair can become more porous, making it more prone to dryness, frizz, breakage and overall damage.

THE PROCEDURE

Keratin treatments work by coating the hair with a protein-rich formula that helps smooth the hair cuticle and improve its overall appearance.

During the treatment, a keratin-based hair-smoothing product is carefully applied to the hair and then sealed using a flat iron. This helps the product penetrate and smooth the hair, leaving it looking softer, shinier and more manageable.

✦

The treatment time can vary depending on your hair length, thickness and texture.

IS AFTERCARE IMPORTANT?

Absolutely! Proper aftercare plays an important role in helping you maintain the results of your keratin treatment for longer. One of the best choices after a keratin treatment is to avoid sulphates.

Sulphate-free shampoos and haircare products are gentler on the hair and can help retain moisture while supporting the smooth, healthy-looking finish of your treatment. With the right aftercare routine and regular professional maintenance, you can keep your hair looking smoother, softer and more manageable for longer.

A SMOOTH FINISH THAT LASTS

A keratin treatment can be a valuable step toward achieving hair that feels more polished, cared for and easier to style. With professional guidance and a routine suited to your individual hair needs, you can enjoy a beautiful, refined finish while keeping your hair looking its best. For the most suitable treatment and long-lasting results, a professional consultation is always the best place to begin.
