SEMI PERMANENT MAKEUP

Fill in the Gaps

Realistic strokes blend into your brows for a natural, fuller shape that stays defined for months with basic care.

INTRODUCTION THE PROCEDURE AFTERCARE IS IMPORTANT CONFIDENCE IN EVERY BROW

Well-shaped eyebrows can make a big difference to the way your face looks. Microblading is a semi-permanent brow treatment that creates fine, natural-looking hair strokes to give your eyebrows a fuller and more defined appearance.

It can be a great option for anyone with sparse, uneven or over-plucked brows, or for those who simply want to spend less time filling in their eyebrows every day. The goal is not to create an artificial look, but to enhance your natural brow shape while keeping the result soft and balanced.

THE PROCEDURE

Microblading is done by carefully creating tiny hair-like strokes on the brow area using a specialised microblading tool and pigment. Before starting, the brow shape is mapped out according to your facial features, natural brow growth and the look you prefer.

Once the shape is finalised, the pigment is gently placed into the skin to create realistic-looking strokes. The procedure is carried out with attention to detail, as even small changes in brow shape can make a noticeable difference to the overall appearance of the face.

✦

The appointment usually takes around 1.5 to 2.5 hours, depending on the consultation, brow mapping and individual requirements.

IS AFTERCARE IMPORTANT?

Absolutely! Proper aftercare is an important part of getting the best results from your microblading treatment. After the procedure, your brows need some time to heal. It is important to keep the area clean and dry as advised by your beauty professional and avoid rubbing, picking or scratching the treated area. Excessive sweating, swimming, steam and direct sun exposure should also be avoided during the initial healing period.

Your brows may appear slightly darker at first, but the colour gradually softens as the skin heals. A follow-up or touch-up appointment may be recommended after the healing period to refine the shape and colour where needed. With the right aftercare and professional maintenance, microblading can help you enjoy naturally fuller, well-defined and beautifully shaped brows for longer.

CONFIDENCE IN EVERY BROW

Microblading is more than a beauty enhancement. It can help bring harmony and definition to your overall look while complementing your individual features. Choosing an experienced professional and following personalised guidance throughout the process can make the experience comfortable and rewarding, leaving you with brows that beautifully frame your face and suit your personal style.
