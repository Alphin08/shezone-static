GOOD TO KNOW

Client Instructions for Bridal & Make-up Services

01

Face Makeup

  • Please share reference images of preferred hairstyles in advance and discuss them with your stylist.
  • Avoid new facials, chemical peels, waxing, threading, laser treatments, or exfoliation on the face at least 48 hours prior to the appointment.
  • Please inform your makeup artist in advance if you have allergies, sensitive skin, active acne, pigmentation, rosacea, eczema, or have recently undergone any dermatological or aesthetic treatments.
  • Kindly share reference images in advance (makeup, hairstyle, outfit, jewellery, etc.) to help us understand your expectations and finalise the look efficiently.
  • Ensure adequate sleep the night before and stay well hydrated for a few days prior to the event to enhance skin appearance.
  • Avoid alcohol consumption and excessive salt intake at least 24 hours before the event to minimise puffiness and dehydration.
  • Makeup outcomes may vary depending on skin condition, weather, lighting, and individual skin response.
02

On the Day of the Appointment

  • Please try to arrive on time with a clean face, free from heavy creams, oils, or makeup.
  • Also make sure to eat before the makeup session begins to avoid discomfort during the service.
  • Do not forget to bring all fresh flowers, accessories, hair ornaments, and jewellery as discussed with our team in advance.
03

Saree Draping

  • Please ensure the draping style is discussed in advance with your stylist and that the saree is brought well ahead of the appointment time.
  • If complete ironing is required, it will be charged additionally
  • Kindly note that we do not provide ironing services for sarees made of delicate fabrics such as pure silk, organza, net, georgette, or sarees with heavy embellishments or embroidery. Sarees made from these fabrics must be brought fully ironed and neatly rolled, ready for draping.
  • At She Zone Beauty Lounge & Spa, we offer the following saree preparations:
     ->Pre-pleated, ironed, and box-folded sarees
     ->Puffed pleated, ironed sarees on hangers
     ->Ironed and rolled sarees.
  • For pricing, please consult our front office team.
